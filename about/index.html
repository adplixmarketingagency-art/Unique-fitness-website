Name, floor, hours.

Written from what the public listing and the gym's own photographs confirm. Nothing on this page asks you to take anything else on faith.

We opened Unique Unisex Fitness and Gym in 2025, in Kumaran Nagar near Mudaliarpet, set back on 100 Feet Road. One floor: no annexe, no rented corner, and a frontage that carries its name in its own light, which is why nobody has to explain where it is after dark.

"Unisex" sits on the sign beside the name because that is what the floor actually is: men and women on the same equipment, at whichever listed hour suits them. The doors run from 05:00 to 22:00, every single day, so a morning shift and a night shift both get their window.

The public listing names what we run on: gym access, CrossFit, yoga, meditation, and "Get Your Own Trainer" for one-to-one work. As useful as the list is what it leaves out. Membership rates are not published anywhere yet; for that question the honest answer is one phone call, and someone at the desk will give it straight.

Everything on this site was checked against the listing and the photographs above. If it isn't confirmed, the page doesn't claim it. Good training habit, applied to a website.

Built on the competition floor.

Scroll through the founder's story from the competition stage to the gym floor. Each chapter pairs the original photograph with the achievement behind it.

  1. Mr. India · 2017

    New Delhi · Bodybuilding · 60 kg category

    On the national stage, the founder won the 60 kg bodybuilding category at Mr. India 2017 in New Delhi. The gold medal marked the beginning of a record built through discipline, preparation, and complete focus.

    Gold medal · National title

  2. Mr. Tamil Nadu · 2017

    Chennai · Overall championship

    The same year, the journey continued at the state level. In Chennai, he was named the overall champion at Mr. Tamil Nadu 2017 — a title that showed the national win was part of a larger standard, not a single moment.

    Overall champion · State title

  3. Mr. Asia · 2019

    Pondicherry · International bodybuilding stage

    In 2019, the founder stepped onto the Mr. Asia stage in Pondicherry and earned a bronze medal. It was the next level of the same pursuit: carry the work further, compete with confidence, and represent home on an international platform.

    Bronze medal · International stage

  4. Two titles · 2019

    Mr. India 2017 · Mr. Asia 2019

    This portrait holds the two titles that define the founder's competitive chapter: first Mr. India and first Mr. Asia. Those milestones are more than lines on a page — they are the standard he brings into every training session.

    A competition legacy carried forward

  5. The work continues

    Training became a daily practice

    Competition gave the founder a standard. Training gave him a daily practice. Away from the stage, the work stayed simple and demanding: return to the gym, build strength, and keep the promise made to yourself.

    Consistency over applause

  6. Pillars of Puducherry

    Pondicherry · Community recognition

    The story grew beyond competition. Recognition as one of the Pillars of Puducherry reflects a wider role: an athlete whose discipline, example, and commitment have become part of the community around him.

    A local story with a larger reach

  7. From record to responsibility

    Unique Fitness Gym · Mudaliarpet

    Today, that competition mindset is the foundation of Unique Fitness: a place where anyone can train with structure, consistency, and intent. The medals tell the history. The gym is where that history becomes useful to someone else.

    Train with purpose · Build your own chapter

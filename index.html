Started in 2025.

A new gym in an old neighbourhood, in its own voice.

We opened Unique Unisex Fitness and Gym in 2025 on 100 Feet Road, in Kumaran Nagar, Mudaliarpet. One floor, lit every evening, doors on the main road; you cannot mistake it for anything else after dark.

“Unisex” is on the sign next to our name because that is what this floor is: men and women training in the same space, at whatever hour suits them. The gates open at 05:00 and we shut at 22:00, every single day of the week.

The public listing names the options: gym access, CrossFit, yoga, meditation, and “Get Your Own Trainer” for one-to-one work. Sessions and schedules move week to week; the honest answer about any of them is one phone call.

Our first members keep saying the same things on Google: cleanliness is maintained well, new sets every day, the training was very effective. That review average, 5.0 from twenty reviews, belongs to them, not to us.

The plan is not complicated: show up, train properly under someone who knows your name, and leave the floor better than you found it.

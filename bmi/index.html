Healthy weight

Height and weight are worked to one number.

The bands

Underweight under 18.5

Small for the frame, or the weight is still dropping. Building food plus resistance training is the usual route.

Healthy weight 18.5 – 24.9

The band the calculator stops on. The aim here is keeping it while strength builds.

Overweight 25 – 29.9

Above the band. Regular cardio plus strength work brings it down at a sensible pace.

Obese 30 and up

Well above the band. Start lighter and let a coach set the plan; going slow is the fast way here.

Bands follow the World Health Organization's global table. For South Asian adults, medical guidance flags risk earlier, from around BMI 23.

BMI is a rough screen: it does not read muscle or fat apart. The desk reads it with you.

Calories for the next move.

Weight alone is not enough for a useful estimate. Add age, sex, height and activity to get maintenance, a gentle deficit and a small gain target.
